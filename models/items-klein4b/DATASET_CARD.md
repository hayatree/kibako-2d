# kibako items-klein4b data

262 synthetic game item icons (132 generic items, such as `an iron sword` or `a treasure chest`) used to train [`ttreetoshi/kibako-items-klein4b`](https://huggingface.co/ttreetoshi/kibako-items-klein4b).

- Generated with [`black-forest-labs/FLUX.2-klein-4B`](https://huggingface.co/black-forest-labs/FLUX.2-klein-4B) (Apache-2.0) at revision `e7b7dc27f91deacad38e78976d1f2b499d76a294`, prompt `game item icon of {item}, single object, centered, plain white background`, 1024 px, 4 steps. Four candidates per item were checked automatically (white background, margins, subject size) and then hand-picked.
- Each image has a `.txt` caption (the bare item name) and a row in `manifest.jsonl` recording its SHA-256, the generator, its exact weights revision, the prompt, the seed and the license.
- These are the exact images the released LoRA (step 1500 of run `kibako_items_klein4b_v1`) was trained on.
